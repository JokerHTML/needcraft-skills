# Worked example: 60-second Shorts script

Topic: three mistakes that kill houseplants.
Word count: 147 words. Estimated duration at 150 wpm: ~59 seconds.
Verify with the read-aloud test before recording. Replace the topic
and the steps with your own — this file shows the shape, not content
to copy.

## On-screen headline (0–3s)

"Your plant is dying. You did this."

## Voiceover (full text)

"Your plant was fine when you brought it home. Now the leaves are yellow, and you have no idea why. Step one: stop watering on a schedule — water only when the top two centimetres of soil are dry. A calendar does not know your plant.

Step two: move it out of the dark corner — most houseplants need bright light that never touches the leaves directly. If you cannot read a book there at noon, it is too dark.

Step three: stop repotting a struggling plant — new soil does not fix overwatering. Fix the water first, then wait two weeks.

One honest limit: a plant with rotted roots cannot be saved by any trick. If the roots are black and soft, start again with a cutting.

Save this for the next time a leaf turns yellow — that is the moment you will need it."

## Structure check

- Hook 0–3s: "Your plant is dying. You did this."
- Proof 3–15s: the three mistakes, and the fix for each.
- Steps 15–45s: three numbered steps.
- Caveat 45–55s: rotted roots cannot be saved.
- Call to action 55–60s: save it.
- Save-worthy asset: the three fixes.
- Future-moment anchor: "the next time a leaf turns yellow".
